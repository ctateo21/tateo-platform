DPA calculation logic:
Normal FHA:
- Down payment = 3.5%
- No DPA second lien
- First mortgage loan amount = purchase price - 3.5% down payment
- Use normal FHA rate/pricing
- FHA upfront MIP stays the same
- FHA monthly MIP stays the same

FHA DPA:
- Down payment shown to user = 0%
- DPA amount = 3.5% of purchase price
- First mortgage base loan amount should effectively be the FHA loan amount before UFMIP based on 96.5% LTV unless the current app already calculates FHA differently.
- FHA upfront MIP stays the same.
- FHA monthly MIP stays the same.
- Only the interest rate changes based on the DPA pricing adjustments below.
- The user should see that DPA increases the payment/rate compared with normal FHA.

Repayable DPA:
- DPA amount = 3.5% of purchase price
- Add a second lien payment to the monthly mortgage calculation.
- Second lien term = 10 years / 120 months
- Second lien interest rate = first mortgage rate + 1.00%
- Calculate the monthly second lien payment using the DPA amount, 120-month term, and second lien interest rate.
- Add this second lien payment into the total monthly payment and DTI.
- Display this second lien payment separately in the estimate.

Forgivable DPA / Silent second:
- DPA amount = 3.5% of purchase price
- No monthly second lien payment.
- First mortgage interest rate is higher based on the silent second pricing below.
- Display that the DPA is a silent/forgivable second lien with no monthly payment.
- Add any required discount points to cash to close if applicable based on the pricing below.

DPA FHA pricing adjustments:
Build this into the FHA pricing parameters.

FHA DPA — Repayable 3.5% assistance:
- Credit score 680+:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 0.65%
- Credit score 660–679:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 0.82%
- Credit score 640–659:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 0.95%
- Credit score 620–639:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.10%
- Credit score 600–619:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.32%
- Credit score below 600:
  - DPA is not available

FHA DPA — Forgivable / Silent second:
- Credit score 680+:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.53%
  - Discount points = 0
- Credit score 640–679:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.53%
  - Discount points = 1.00 point
- Credit score 600–639:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.53%
  - Discount points = 1.50 points
- Credit score below 600:
  - DPA is not available

Discount point handling:
- If forgivable DPA requires discount points, calculate points as a percentage of the applicable loan amount.
- Add those points to cash to close.
- Display them clearly as DPA/FHA discount points.
- Do not treat points as down payment.
