Estimator Formula

The cleanest estimate should be based on replacement cost, not sale price.

Estimated Replacement Cost = Living Area Sq Ft × Local Rebuild Cost per Sq Ft

Base Premium = Coverage A × ZIP Premium Factor

Adjusted Premium = Base Premium × Roof × Construction × Wind Mitigation × Opening Protection × Coastal Exposure × Deductible Profile

ZIP Premium Factor = Total Homeowners Premium With Wind ÷ Total Structure Exposure With Wind

Data Structure for ZIP-Level Upgrade

When you import OIR ZIP-level data, structure it like this:

ZIP | County | Premium With Wind | Structure Exposure With Wind | Premium % of Rebuild Cost

Then map each ZIP to its factor:

33602: 0.0125, 33706: 0.0185, 34102: 0.0225
Final premiums depend on carrier eligibility, roof age, wind mitigation inspection, construction type, deductibles, coverage selections, claims history, and underwriting rules. Flood insurance is separate unless specifically included.
