Fix two items in the Purchase Flow FHA DPA / See My Estimate logic.

Item 1 — Forgivable FHA DPA discount points must increase closing costs

Current behavior:
When I choose FHA DPA Repayable or Forgivable, the rate increase is working correctly.

Issue:
For Forgivable FHA DPA, the discount points are not increasing closing costs correctly.

Expected behavior:
If Forgivable FHA DPA has discount points, those points should increase estimated closing costs / cash to close.

Calculation:
Discount points amount = loan amount × discount points percentage

Examples:
- 1 discount point on a $300,000 loan = $3,000 added closing cost
- 1.5 discount points on a $300,000 loan = $4,500 added closing cost

Forgivable FHA DPA pricing reminder:
- Credit score 680+:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.53%
  - Discount points = 0
- Credit score 640–679:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.53%
  - Discount points = 1.00 point
- Credit score 600–639:
  - First mortgage rate = base FHA/MND rate + 1.53%
  - Discount points = 1.50 points
- Credit score below 600:
  - DPA unavailable

Requirements:
1. When Forgivable FHA DPA is selected, calculate the discount points amount.
2. Add the discount points amount to estimated closing costs.
3. Add the discount points amount to cash to close.
4. Display the discount points clearly on the estimate.
5. Do not apply discount points to Repayable DPA unless already intended elsewhere.
6. Do not apply discount points to Standard FHA.
7. Do not change FHA monthly MIP or upfront MIP logic.
8. Do not change the DPA rate increase logic, because that is already working.
9. If the user toggles away from Forgivable DPA, remove the discount points from closing costs/cash to close.
10. If the credit score changes and the discount point tier changes, update closing costs/cash to close immediately.

Suggested fields:
- `dpa_discount_points_percent`
- `dpa_discount_points_amount`
- `estimated_closing_costs_before_dpa_points`
- `estimated_closing_costs`
- `cash_to_close`
