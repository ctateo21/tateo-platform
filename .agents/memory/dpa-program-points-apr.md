---
name: DPA program-points APR allocation
description: Compliance classification for the 2% charge attached to the FHA 5% silent-second option.
---

Treat the 2% DPA program-points charge as a prepaid finance charge of the first mortgage exactly once. Do not allocate it to the no-payment silent second or describe it as a rate-buydown charge.

**Why:** The first-mortgage lender separately imposes this consumer-paid charge as a condition of obtaining the first-lien credit with the 5% assistance option. Under 12 CFR 1026.4(a) and (b)(3), that makes it a finance charge of the first-lien transaction. The silent second does not impose the charge.

**How to apply:** Keep its lender-fee and cash-to-close treatment aligned with its first-lien APR treatment. Any change in program ownership or written terms requires a fresh compliance classification rather than moving or duplicating the charge by assumption.
